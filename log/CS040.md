## P1 — Milestone becomes a conditional health spawn

Measured: the score milestone's healing arm supplied 63% (run A) and 62% (run B) of all healing —
climbing with wave automatically because score rate does, though nobody designed a healing system.
`REPAIR_AMOUNT`/`REPAIR_FULL_BONUS` deleted outright, zero consumers left. Below `SHIP_MAX_HP` a
crossing now calls `spawnHealthPowerup()` (the ambient placement path, reused) and pings; at
exactly `SHIP_MAX_HP` it does nothing and makes no sound. `game.nextRepair` still advances on every
crossing. GDD §2's milestone/health bullets and §2.12's hull-full parenthetical corrected in place.

## P2 — Pity-driven ambient health cadence

`POWERUP_HEALTH_GAP`'s flat `[18, 26]` s roll retired. `healthGapRoll()` lerps
`[HEALTH_GAP_LOW_HURT, HEALTH_GAP_HIGH_HURT]` (6–10 s at zero hull) to
`[HEALTH_GAP_LOW_OK, HEALTH_GAP_HIGH_OK]` (22–30 s at full hull) on `game.ship.hp / SHIP_MAX_HP`.
No re-roll added on the damage path (would let a player farm spawns by tanking hits). Four new
POWERUPS registry knobs; registry 104 → 108, `LEVERS` unmoved.

## P3 — Health banking

Health now BANKS instead of evaporating at the cap. `applyPowerup()`'s health arm applies what the
hull has room for and banks the remainder as a whole charge worth `POWERUP_HEALTH_AMOUNT`
(`game.healthBank`, 0..`DEBUG.healthBankMax`); past the cap the leftover lands in the new cumulative
`game.stats.hpWasted`. One charge auto-spends per damage event at `damageShip()`'s hull-reduction
site, below the death check, so a lethal hit is never rescued. New `AudioSys.bankspend()` cue. HUD
tell: a segmented pip row under the "HULL" label (`drawRingSegments()`), always drawn, stroke-only.
`healthBank` is additive in the save envelope, no schema bump. One new POWERUPS knob; registry
108 → 109.

## P4 — Recycle-hub resupply

The hub's own drop (`game.deliveryCount === 8`) is a supply-starvation escape hatch. `dropPowerup()`
gains an optional `hubBias` parameter, passed `true` only by the hub's own call: while set, any
budgeted type at zero balance (`rapid`/`triple`/`magnet`/`engine`, never `guard`) has its roll
weight multiplied by `DEBUG.hubDryWeightMult` (4). `destroyHunter()`'s and `destroySaucer()`'s drops
pass no fifth argument and stay unbiased — those are kill-gated, and biasing them would defeat the
point. Composes with, does not replace, guard's own eligibility gate and pity-weight substitution.
Registry 109 → 110.

## P5 — Telemetry v4

44 → 49 columns. Out: `scoreRepairBonus`. In: instantaneous `hunterCount`/`debrisCount`/
`garbageCount`/`healthBanked`, cumulative `hpWasted`, and `scoopHits` — the schema's second
sawtooth, zeroed by `damageShip()` on every scoop level loss. New `Telemetry.flush()`, called from
`killShip()`, pushes the death row so a completed run's last row IS the death frame. Header block
7 → 9 lines: `# ringWrapped=` and `# finalRowIsGameOver=`. `TELEMETRY_MAX` 400 → 800,
`TELEMETRY_PERSIST_EVERY` = 4. Envelope `v: 3 → 4`; key name untouched. Registry and `LEVERS` both
unmoved this phase.

## P6 — Options telemetry submenu

Telemetry controls moved off the hidden debug panel onto a new Options "Telemetry" sub-screen:
`MENU_OPTIONS` gains "Telemetry" before "Back" (5 rows → 6). Three rows — Capture (ON/OFF), Sample
rate (preset cycle `[5, 10, 15]`, minutes label derived from `TELEMETRY_MAX`), Copy log. Capture
stays session-only — `DEBUG.telemetryCapture` is still the CS038 P3 `sessionSwitch` registry entry.
Debug panel: `telemetryInterval`/`telemetryCapture` stay in `DEBUG_VARS`/`DEBUG_ENTRIES` (still
read there by `Telemetry`) but their rows carry `hidden: true`, skipped by navigation and render.
"Copy telemetry log" removed from the debug panel outright. Registry and `LEVERS` both unmoved.
