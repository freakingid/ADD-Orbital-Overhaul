## P1 — Delivery ticker re-tune; the milestone floaters deleted

Applied a later `tools/dock-float-lab.html` session's seven values as the shipped defaults,
**superseding CS034 P8's GATE A numbers** — not a contradiction, a second measurement:
`DELIVERY_FLOAT_ANCHOR_FRAC` 0.75 → **0.50**, `deliveryFloatRise` 200 → **150**, `deliveryFloatSize`
18 → **16**, `deliveryFloatSizeStep` 0.0 → **1.0**, `deliveryFloatSizeMax` 36 → **48**,
`deliveryFloatHold` 0.25 → **0.00**, `deliveryFloatFade` 0.75 → **1.20**. `min`/`max`/`step` on all
five registry rows unchanged; `DOCK_OFFLOAD_INTERVAL` stayed 0.05.

`sizeStep` off 0.0 is the substantive one: **CS034 shipped the per-piece growth machinery and then set
its step to zero**, so the ticker never actually grew. CS035 turns the mechanism on for the first
time. At size 16 / step 1.0 / cap 48, a 24-piece haul climbs 16 → 39 px and never reaches the cap —
the cap binds only above 33 pieces, which `CARGO_CAP_MAX` (24) makes unreachable. Intentional
headroom, not a dead knob.

Deleted the **"SALVAGE BONUS"** (first of the 8/12/16/20 reward tiers) and **"MAX HAUL"** (24-piece)
`FloatText` pushes: the lab measured **−15.7 px** ink clearance against the re-tuned ticker — real
overlap, three strings in the same 40 px of screen. Only the two pushes went; the four
`dropPowerup()` reward-tier awards, `game.stats.maxChainVisit`, `game.cargoFlash = HUD_CAP_FLASH`,
`superMegaDelivery()` and its `dock_24` voice line all stayed. That opened **FLAG-CS035-a** for the
gate.

repointed in the same commit, same convention as their own prior "REPOINTED BY" comments

## P2 — Dock scoop lockout; the incidental branch deleted

⛔ **The diagnosis in the request document was close but not the mechanism.** Incidental pickups
inside the ring already did *not* reset `deliveryCount` — the `!inRing` guard at the pickup gate
existed precisely to prevent that, and CS020 P1b's comment said so. What actually happened was the
**LIFO queue jump**: a piece hooked inside the ring was tagged `towed: false` and pushed onto the
chain *tail*, the offload pops the *tail*, so an incidental hooked mid-offload delivered **next** —
ahead of the towed load — and landed in the flat-pay `else` branch. No `deliveryCount++`, no
escalating points, no pitch climb, no ticker growth. **The load was intact; the feedback was not.**

The capture gate now leads with `!inRing`, so nothing can be hooked while the ship is inside the
dock's neighbourhood ring, **at any chain length including zero** (a conditional lockout is not
learnable). A piece that reaches the capture region — base circle **or** scoop mouth — instead has its
velocity **SET**, never added, to the new `dockBounceSpeed` knob (DELIVERY, def 90, 20–300/10) along
the wrap-aware ship→piece vector, with `AudioSys.shieldPing()` as the tell and the ship completely
unaffected. ⛔ `debrisBounce()` is deliberately not called — it is a mass-aware two-body elastic solver
whose contract holds neither a canister nor the ship. The magnet's pull is suppressed in the ring
through `pulling` (the two budget-spend sites still read the raw `magnet`, unchanged — CS025's split
is a standing invariant this phase did not get to relax), so nothing churns against that push.

That makes the incidental category **empty by construction**, so CS020's `towed: !inRing` tag, the
`node.towed !== false` read, the `if (towed)` wrapper and the whole incidental branch (flat
`DOCK_BASE_SCORE`, its size-12 floater, `AudioSys.deliver(1)`) were **deleted** — the actual fix for
the queue-jump stall, which was never a counter reset. ⛔ `inRing` itself survives, hoisted above the
garbage loop, still guarding the pickup gate and the `deliveryCount = 0` reset for a haul started
outside the ring.

Registry 91 → 92; no lever moved. New `scratchpad/test-cs035-p2.js`. Twenty suite files repointed in
the same commit — nineteen phase tests plus `test-registry.js`'s count. The nineteen keep their
scenarios and **invert what they assert** (the park now pays 0 rather than a flat 30,000; a +39
capture is refused rather than tagged), so CS020's claim survives strictly strengthened and no
section was deleted.

**Two phase decisions, recorded because neither was a spec line.** The push's degenerate direction: a
piece resting exactly on the ship's centre has no ship→piece vector, and the house `|| 0.0001` idiom
would hand it a velocity of *zero* — pinned on the hull with coalescence still running. It falls back
to the ship's own facing, so the magnitude is always `dockBounceSpeed`. And `AudioSys.shieldPing()`
fires once per pushed piece per frame, so with several pieces on the hull the tell stacks; no rate
limit was added because §2.3 asked for the shipped ping and no new audio method, and a cooldown is a
design call.

## P3 — Level-end invincibility window

A protection window now spans the level-end seam. `game.levelEndSafe` opens on the frame the last
Garbage Satellite dies (a `waveClearTimer === 0` latch in the wave-clear branch), holds through
`levelEndHold`, the celebration panel, `nextWave()` and the "Level N" banner, and closes when the
post-banner grace — armed as a one-shot on the banner's expiry **crossing** — reaches exactly 0. The
`2.5` hold literal was **replaced** by `DEBUG.levelEndHold` (5.0 s total, not 7.5). Four new
CELEBRATION knobs: `levelEndHold` 5.00, `levelEndGrace` 3.00, `levelEndFade` 0.25,
`levelEndGracePulseEnd` 0.08 — the last two in the same units as each other on purpose (both are
one-way 100 % → 20 % transition times; `levelEndGracePulseEnd` was offered in conversation as a
*period* and is specified as a transition time so a mixed pair two rows apart can never be read
against each other and be wrong by 2×).

Five spec gate sites became **four** literal edits: `&& !game.levelEndSafe` on the two
`ship.invuln <= 0` conditions, and a new guard on each of the two chain blocks — **guarded, not
absorbed**, so `breakChain()` is never entered and no chain-guard charge is spent. `ship.invuln`,
`shieldDeflect`/`shieldBounce`, `SHIELD_HIT_COST`, `dmgThisWave` and the Perfect Wave latch are all
untouched.

The tell is a triangle wave on ship alpha (1.0 → 0.2 → 1.0, phase accumulated in **half-cycles**) that
**replaces** the hit-stun blink for the window's duration and accelerates across the grace.

Registry 92 → 96; no lever moved. New `scratchpad/test-cs035-p3.js`. Seventeen suite files repointed
plus `test-registry.js`'s count.

**Three things the phase found that the spec had not:**

1. **A wave cleared while the PREVIOUS level's banner is still live arms the grace early.** The
   banner-expiry one-shot fires on any crossing with `levelEndSafe` true and cannot distinguish the
   level-1 banner it was written to exclude from a level-N banner that has simply not expired yet.
   Unreachable at the shipped 2.2 s `levelBannerTime` (a level-1 clear is ~39 kills), but that knob
   goes to 8 s. Narrowing the arm is a design call, so it was flagged rather than taken.
2. **Spec §3.6's five gate sites are four `if`s in the shipped build.** Sites 2 and 3 are the same
   condition — the Hunter arm (`damageShip()` plus CS023 P3's mutual kill) lives *inside* the
   hazards-vs-ship block, whose own `invuln <= 0` gate its comment already names as the rate limiter.
   One guard covers both rows.
3. **`nextWave()` had no "reset block comment" to append to.** The text the prompt described is
   actually `buildSaveEntry()`'s "Deliberately absent" note; `nextWave()` zeroes only `waveTime` and
   `stats.dmgThisWave`. The required ⛔ note was written fresh above those two. The three new fields'
   reset went into `resetRun()`, not `startGame()` — the standing CS016 P3 both-places rule, and what
   "startGame() ONLY" means today, since a field added to `startGame()`'s thin body would be missed by
   every resumed run.

## P4 — Hunter volatility: the clock and the tell

Spec §4.1–§4.3 only; damage sources were P5's. `HunterSatellite.age` accrues from **construction**,
unconditionally, every tier, every `update(dt)` — construction is the only spawn event a large core
has (`static spawnCore()` died in CS024 P3). `volatile()` is
`size === 3 && age >= DEBUG.hunterVolatileAge`; mediums and smalls age but never read it.

While volatile, `pulseScale` (init 100) grows at `hunterPulseGrow` %/s and shrinks at
`hunterPulseShrink` %/s — asymmetric, fast up / slow down, so it reads as pumping rather than
breathing — clamped at `hunterPulseMin`/`hunterPulseMax` with a direction flip on each clamp.
⛔ `draw()` scales a **fresh** vertex array by `pulseScale/100` per frame; `this.shape`/`this.inner`/
`this.radius` are never touched, so the collision hitbox stays honest against animation phase.

Five new HUNTER-section knobs (registry 96 → 101), **none a lever** — `DIFFICULTY-LEVERS.md` gained a
not-a-lever row, `LEVERS` stays 18. New `scratchpad/test-cs035-p4.js`. Twelve older suite files
pinning the registry's order/count against a fixed parent SHA were repointed for the five new rows.

## P5 — Hunter volatility: damage sources

Spec §4.4. A **volatile large** Hunter is now destructible by three new sources, all via
`destroyHunter(h, false)` — no score, no achievement counters, 3-way split unchanged (⛔
`ACH_LINEAGE_FULL = 13` depends on that split staying 3-way).

- **Hunter↔Hunter** — a new pair walk over `game.hunters`, same slot in the collision order as the
  Debris-vs-Debris pass. Both must be volatile larges or nothing happens; both die if so.
- **Hostile bullet** — a new `game.hunters` loop in the hostile-bullet branch, volatile larges only,
  bullet dies on contact. ⛔ A **sibling** of the `!game.levelEndSafe`-guarded chain block, deliberately
  *not* covered by that guard: a UFO shot still pops a volatile large during the level-end window.
- **Saucer body** — the UFO-vs-Debris pass gains a Hunter arm; unlike the Debris arm, **both** die, no
  bounce.

Non-volatile larges, mediums and smalls are unaffected by all three; player bullets are unchanged
(regression-tested). No registry rows this phase.

New `scratchpad/test-cs035-p5.js` drives the real collision passes end to end. **Trap staged for:**
`game.debris.length === 0` arms a large core's last-stand steer-toward-ship drift in its own
`update()`, which would move a "stationary" large off its staged overlap before collisions run — every
scenario keeps one inert sentinel Debris object in the field (the `quiet()` idiom from
`test-cs035-p4.js`) so that branch never arms; and world size at level 1 is the small early-level
world (1920×1080), not the 2560×1440 default, so hand-placed positions are read off `worldDims(X)`
live rather than from a literal.

Two pre-existing phase-local pins broke as a *direct, expected* consequence and were repointed, not
redesigned: `test-cs023-p2.js` §A's "only closing braces between the debris pass and whichever runs
next" now checks for the new Hunter↔Hunter pass first (it legitimately landed in that slot), and
`test-cs023-p3.js` §A's `destroySaucer(s, false)` call-site count moved 2 → 3.

## P6 — Powerup rebalance

Spec §5, two independent fixes on two separate complaints.

**`guard` gets rarer and conditional.** `POWERUP_DROP_WEIGHTS` ×10 — every non-guard ratio
byte-identical, just scaled, so there is finally room between the values. `guard`'s own entry is now a
placeholder overwritten at roll time by `guardDropWeight()` =
`min(chainGuardDropMax, chainGuardDropBase + chainGuardDropPity × game.stats.cargoDamageEvents)`,
three new CHAIN GUARD knobs (def 4 / 8 / 40): ~3.8 % of a 100 non-guard total at rest, 11 % after one
cargo-damage event, capped ~29 % from four. `cargoDamageEvents` increments in `breakChain()`'s **sever
path only** (a guarded absorb and `scatterChain()` do not count), resets to 0 the instant
`dropPowerup()` *selects* `"guard"` — not on pickup, or a player could farm the counter by ignoring
drops — and carries across waves within a run (reset in `startGame()`'s `resetGameStats()`, ⛔ not
`nextWave()`). `dropPowerup()`'s `weightOf(k)` indirection composes the dynamic weight with the
existing `chainGuardMinTow` eligibility gate; an ineligible key still skips in both the total and the
walk. ⛔ `POWERUP_DROP_TYPES` (the budgeted-effect list) is untouched, per the standing trap.

**The Super Mega Delivery flood shrinks.** `guard` leaves the guaranteed set (6 → 5 types, derived by
`filter`, so the budget reservation follows automatically) and is **re-added explicitly** — flat,
ungated — to the per-piece sweep pool so it stays reachable during a sweep. Two POWERUPS knobs replace
frozen consts: `sweepPowerupCap` (was `SWEEP_POWERUP_CAP` 48, now def **24**) and `dockPowerupSpeed`
(was `DOCK_POWERUP_SPEED` 120, now def **180**, two call sites — the SMD guaranteed set and the
8/12/16/20 reward-tier drops, which move together because both emitters launch from the dock and the
complaint was powerups landing on a parked ship).

Registry 101 → 106; no lever moved. New `scratchpad/test-cs035-p6.js`. Twenty older suite files
pinning weight ratios, the guaranteed-set size, `DOCK_POWERUP_SPEED`/`SWEEP_POWERUP_CAP` call sites,
or the registry's count/order against a fixed parent SHA were repointed in the same commit.

### Deferred by this phase — design decisions, not values

- **G18 — a red Hunter while volatile.** The heartbeat alone did not read as "about to go off." A
  colour change is exactly what `PLANNED-FEATURES-CS035.md` §6 excluded ("**No Hunter colour change**
  for volatility. Motion is the tell."), so it is **not built here**. `hunterVolatileAge` 30 → 60 is
  the only part of that answer applied. Carried into `STATUS.md` as a known issue for a future
  changeset — it is a small, well-scoped piece of work, but it is a design reversal and belongs in a
  plan doc, not a closing phase.
- **G9's bug report — the level-end window appears to do nothing in play.** Paul reported that
  `levelEndHold` "did not seem to do anything," that the Achievements screen arrives immediately at
  level end, that the `levelEndGrace` items do not appear to be in effect, and that he sees no visible
  difference at level end / panel / level start — and therefore declined to answer G9–G14. **This
  phase did not attempt a fix**, and the shipped code was re-read rather than assumed: the arm, the
  hold, the grace one-shot, the four damage gates, the phase accumulator and `Ship.draw()`'s alpha
  block all match spec §3 line for line, and `test-cs035-p3.js` (112 assertions, driving the real
  `update()`/`Ship.draw()` end to end) passes on this commit — including the specific claims that
  nothing happens at 2.6 s, the wave advances at 5.0 s, a retuned 1.0 s hold moves the seam, and ship
  alpha reads 1.0 at phase 0 and 0.2 at phase 1. **The report is not reproducible headlessly and is
  not explained**, so it is carried into `STATUS.md` as an open, unresolved playtest report with the
  specific things to check next session, rather than being written off or guessed at.
- **G5's bug report — Debris sometimes not bouncing away at the dock.** Paul: "Sometimes when player
  ship is in the dock, and hits a piece of debris, the debris is not bouncing away, which causes
  numerous rapid collisions between ship and debris, until the player actually flies the ship out of
  the way." The push's magnitude, direction, set-not-add property and no-accumulation behaviour are
  all pinned in `test-cs035-p2.js` §B and pass, and the knob answer came back at the shipped 90, so
  nothing was retuned. The "numerous rapid collisions" reading is consistent with P2's own known
  issue — `AudioSys.shieldPing()` fires once per pushed piece per frame, so several pieces on the hull
  stack the tell. Carried as an open playtest report; a rate limit on the ping and any change to the
  push's re-entry behaviour are both design calls.
