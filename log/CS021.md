**THE GEOMETRY CLAMP HOLDS THE OUTER EDGE FIXED, NOT THE STEP, AND THAT CHOICE IS LOAD-BEARING.** `orbitRadiusStepFor(count)` redistributes the SAME outer radius the shipped 4-ring geometry reaches (`ORBIT_INNER_RADIUS + (ORBIT_RING_COUNT − 1) × ORBIT_RADIUS_STEP` = 630, fixed regardless of the debug knob) evenly across `count − 1` gaps, rather than deriving from the ~700 px wrap-clean budget directly. At the shipped default (`count === 4`) this reproduces `ORBIT_RADIUS_STEP` (150) **bit-for-bit** — verified, not assumed — which is what keeps P1/P2's pinned geometry table (counts 6/6/7/21, outer edge 676) from moving a pixel unless the knob is actually touched. At `count 3` the step widens to 225; at a requested `count 5` it would narrow to 112.5, below the 132 px floor (`DEBRIS_RADII[3]*2 + ORBIT_RADIUS_STEP_PAD`), which is exactly what `orbitEffectiveCount` walks back down from 5 to 4.
