The report is "clunky" and "not smooth". That is a judgement about pacing, and pacing cannot be guessed into constants — so §3.2/§3.3 name five suspects instead. Watch for each, then set the beats until it stops happening.
  1. Two indefinite gates in a row — the announcement, then the panel, whenever anything unlocked. U shows the sequence with and without.
  2. The panel is a hard cut at both ends. There is no fade anywhere in it.
  3. The freeze lifts mid-banner — the field moves under a label still being read.
  4. The announcement waits forever. No auto-advance, by design.
  5. Game over arrives in one frame — title, score, ten rows and footer together, under a hard-cut modal that is already covering them.
