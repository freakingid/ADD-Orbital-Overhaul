The lowest glow-to-background contrast ratio across the eight edge probes; the centre probe is a control and is excluded. This is the number GATE A A2 is argued in. There is no standard threshold here — WCAG's 3:1 / 4.5:1 bars are for reading TEXT, not for noticing a large soft field in peripheral vision, and quoting one would be inventing a bar. Argue in the ×today multiple, and check it against the canvases above.

Luminance is WCAG 2.x relative luminance (sRGB de-gamma, then 0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B) and the ratio is (Lhi+0.05)/(Llo+0.05) — see the comment above relLum() for why that formula and not a bare luminance ratio. Every figure is sampled with getImageData off a composited 1280×720 render (backdrop → glow → HUD furniture, the game's own order), at the two exact pulse poses, not computed from the alpha arithmetic. The measurement pass is deterministic and independent of the animation: it does not read whatever phase the canvases above happen to be at.

Low-HP Glow Lab

Instrument for the low-hull corner glow (CS038 §2, GATE A). Not part of the game build — no imports, no build step, opens by double-click. The glow itself is the PORT-ME BLOCK, copied verbatim out of drawHUD() and driven from the sliders below; the backdrop and HUD furniture are lab-only mocks at the build's real coordinates.

Compare

Side-by-side reads brightness; flip-in-place reads it far better — the same screen pixels swap between the two parameter sets, so the eye compares against its own afterimage instead of across 12px of gutter.

Hull state

t = 1 − hp/100. The default is HP 100, i.e. t = 0 — the moment the alarm ENGAGES, where it is weakest and where "I never noticed it" is most likely to be true. At t = 0 peak alpha is ALPHA_MIN and the trough is ALPHA_MIN × 0.2 (0.02 as shipped). Walk the slider to 1 to see near-death.

Unlock to watch the two rates beat against each other; leave locked (on the candidate's rate) to judge brightness without a phase difference confounding it.

Candidate — shape

Shape changes are a draw-block change in P6, not a constants retune. Whatever wins stays a FILL, stays gated on game.lowHpSiren, stays driven by game.lowHpPhase, and adds no shadowBlur — CS037's benchmark put ctx.shadowBlur at the top of the renderer's cost. No shape in this lab uses one.

Candidate — values

⚠ off the COLOR.lowhp mirror (255,64,64 = #ff4040). The glow shares its colour with the critical-hull ring on purpose, so moving it is a deliberate GATE A A5 answer, not a side effect of dragging a slider.

⚠ The two rate knobs are not glow knobs. lowhpPulseRate(t) is the ONE formula the audio siren's LFO and the critical-hull ring read as well, so moving either moves all three. They are here because the glow cannot be judged without them — not because P6 may quietly retune them.

The 0.6 + 0.4·sin pulse split is a literal inside the shipped block and is deliberately not a slider: changing it would be a draw-block edit, not a constants retune, and it would put the port out of sync with the build. Its consequence is measured instead — that is what the peak:trough column is.
